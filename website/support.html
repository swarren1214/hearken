Email support@hearthapps.com. If you’re reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version, Hearken version, and the steps to reproduce it. Please don’t email passwords or sensitive personal information.

Common questions

How does iCloud sync work?

Hearken can sync study data through your private iCloud account. Check that you’re signed in to iCloud on your device and that iCloud is available for Hearken. Sync depends on your Apple Account, network connection, and iCloud settings. Some data remains on your device when iCloud is unavailable.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Sign in with Apple is optional. You can use Hearken without signing in to the app. iCloud sync availability is controlled separately by your device’s iCloud settings.

How do I change reading and display settings?

Open Hearken Settings to adjust appearance, scripture text size, verse numbers, reader layout, highlight colors, listening preferences, daily goal, and study reminders.

How do I delete my account or study data?

Use the account or data deletion option in Hearken Settings. Deletion may also sync to your other devices when iCloud is enabled. Signing out alone does not delete your study data. For help, email support@hearthapps.com.
