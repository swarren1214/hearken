# Sources, provenance, and reuse dispositions

Source identity checked on 2026-10-10. Scripture locators are checked against the local Hearken text and generated canonical URL pattern; external source identities were checked through web pages or public search. This is not a bulk network link test, a doctrinal endorsement, or proof of YouTube playback. Publisher metadata verifies book identity, not every argument in the book.

No copied modern article, transcript, recording, or paywalled book text is included. Original Hearken prose and activities are the in-app content; outside sources retain their own rights.

| Disposition | App treatment |
|---|---|
| Original Hearken material | Include lesson prose, prompts, learning items, and rubrics from this package. |
| existing_app_text | Render the assigned selection through the app’s scripture reader. Audit edition rights separately. |
| link_only | Open the source or a permitted hosted reader. Do not ingest full text without permission. |
| provider_embed_only | Use the provider’s official player when permitted; keep external link and original accessible alternative. No download or rehosting. |
| public_domain_US | Candidate for original-text import in the US after edition/provenance checks. Check distribution territories, later additions, and Gutenberg packaging conditions. Currently linked only. |
| recommendation_only | Display bibliographic recommendation and link. Never make purchase a progress gate. |

## Editorial stance

This course represents The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, not every denomination in the broader Latter Day Saint movement. LDS institutional sources describe that Church’s position. Catholic, Orthodox, and Lutheran sources describe their own traditions. Institutional faith claims are not presented as scholarly consensus. Modern academic recommendations are interpretive scholarship and not official doctrine. Historical commentary needs date and context. Difficult history is taught with accurate attribution and without requiring personal disclosure.
