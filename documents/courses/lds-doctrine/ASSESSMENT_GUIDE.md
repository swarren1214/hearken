# LDS Doctrine — facilitator and assessment guide

Learner-facing curriculum: CURRICULUM.md. This file contains answer guides; do not automatically display it beside graded questions. Evaluate equivalent explanations rather than exact wording. Do not grade assent, membership, family status, donations, or private spiritual experiences.

## Scoring and completion

Required lesson: accept a relevant short summary with a source and an interpretive limit. Required reading: accept a sourced answer substantially satisfying its guide; invite correction for major errors. Practice: accept only when the explicit success criteria are met. These are reviewed submissions, not checkboxes.

Checkpoint: each of ten answers earns 0–2; pass 16/20. Advancement: each of five answers earns 0–2; pass 8/10. 0 = absent or incorrect; 1 = partially accurate or insufficiently supported; 2 = accurate, contextual, and addresses the requested distinction. Unlimited retries. Answer guides identify the expected reasoning, not a mandatory personal belief.

## Course 1: Learning to Read LDS Doctrine

### Reading guides

**Sources, authority, and careful claims**

- lds-doctrine-c01-m01-reading-01 — Articles of Faith 1:1–4. Prompt: Which topics belong to the opening four articles, and which questions do they leave unanswered? Guide: Identify Godhead, individual accountability, Christ’s Atonement, and foundational principles and ordinances; avoid treating a compact summary as a complete theology.
- lds-doctrine-c01-m01-optional-reading-01 — Joseph Smith—History 1:15–20. Prompt: What theological claims does this narrated account make? Guide: Attribute the claims to the narrative, distinguish event and record dates, and use the documentary accounts collection for historical comparison.

**The Godhead and the language of unity**

- lds-doctrine-c01-m02-reading-01 — Doctrine and Covenants 130:22–23. Prompt: What distinctions does this passage make, and what does it not explain? Guide: Identify the Father and Son as having bodies and the Holy Ghost as a personage of spirit; do not infer an exhaustive account of divine metaphysics.
- lds-doctrine-c01-m02-reading-02 — Godhead: opening doctrinal explanation. Prompt: How does this institutional explanation use unity? Guide: Explain unity of purpose and distinct persons; attribute the explanation to the Church.

### Course checkpoint

1. A popular devotional author states a new theory of divine life. What evidence is needed before calling it official doctrine?
   - Guide: Identify commentary as commentary; consult appropriate canonical and current institutional sources; do not generalize from popularity.
   - Sources: [Approaching Latter-day Saint Doctrine](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/approaching-mormon-doctrine)

2. A passage is canonical, but a proposed interpretation is not explicitly stated in it. How should the claim be labeled?
   - Guide: Distinguish the authoritative text from the reader’s interpretation and give the reasoning and its limit.
   - Sources: [Approaching Latter-day Saint Doctrine](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/approaching-mormon-doctrine)

3. Why does a document’s date matter when describing current teaching?
   - Guide: It locates the claim historically; verify current teaching separately rather than assuming unchanged status.
   - Sources: [Approaching Latter-day Saint Doctrine](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/approaching-mormon-doctrine)

4. Give the four fields a beginner’s source ledger should contain.
   - Guide: Claim, source, setting, and confidence; equivalent fields are acceptable if they preserve attribution and limits.
   - Sources: [Approaching Latter-day Saint Doctrine](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/approaching-mormon-doctrine)

5. What topics appear in the first four Articles of Faith?
   - Guide: Godhead, individual accountability, Christ’s Atonement, and foundational principles and ordinances.
   - Sources: [Articles of Faith 1:1–4](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/a-of-f/1?lang=eng&id=p1-p4#p1)

6. What does D&C 130 distinguish about Father, Son, and Holy Ghost?
   - Guide: Father and Son have bodies; the Holy Ghost is a personage of spirit. State what is explicit without a complete metaphysical theory.
   - Sources: [Doctrine and Covenants 130:22–23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/130?lang=eng&id=p22-p23#p22)

7. Why is “unity” not necessarily equivalent to “the same person”?
   - Guide: Explain that a relationship of purpose or action can unite distinct persons; identify the meaning claimed by the source.
   - Sources: [Godhead](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/godhead?lang=eng)

8. What is wrong with using “God wearing three masks” as a definition of the Nicene Trinity?
   - Guide: It describes interchangeable appearances rather than the tradition’s distinct persons; avoid importing it as a fair comparison.
   - Sources: [Catechism of the Catholic Church, §§232–267](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P17.HTM)

9. Rewrite “Every Latter-day Saint believes exactly this” as a defensible source claim.
   - Guide: Use attributed language such as “This institutional source teaches…” and limit the claim to what the source supports.
   - Sources: [Approaching Latter-day Saint Doctrine](https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/article/approaching-mormon-doctrine)

10. Can a learner earn course credit while disagreeing with a doctrine? Explain the assessment standard.
   - Guide: Yes; accurate explanation, evidence, and reasoning demonstrate learning. Assent or private spiritual experience is not assessed.
   - Sources: Original course design / source-analysis method.

## Course 2: The Plan and the Doctrine of Christ

### Reading guides

**The plan of salvation and moral agency**

- lds-doctrine-c02-m01-reading-01 — Moses 1:39. Prompt: What purpose is stated, and why is it not a complete sequence of the plan? Guide: Identify immortality and eternal life as God’s work and glory; the verse gives purpose rather than a detailed chronology.
- lds-doctrine-c02-m01-reading-02 — 2 Nephi 2:25–27. Prompt: How are joy, redemption, and agency related in Lehi’s teaching? Guide: Describe mortality and choice in relation to the Messiah; do not reduce agency to unrestricted independence.

**The doctrine of Christ as a lived beginning**

- lds-doctrine-c02-m02-reading-01 — 2 Nephi 31:10–21. Prompt: Why does this passage move beyond entering the path? Guide: Identify following Christ, faith, repentance, baptism, receiving the Spirit, and continued steadfastness; explain continuing discipleship.
- lds-doctrine-c02-m02-optional-reading-01 — Alma 36:16–21. Prompt: How does the narrator describe repentance and relief? Guide: Trace recollection of Christ, appeal, and relief; do not require every person to have an identical emotional sequence.

### Course checkpoint

1. What purpose does Moses 1:39 state?
   - Guide: God’s work and glory concern immortality and eternal life; distinguish purpose from complete chronology.
   - Sources: [Moses 1:39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/1?lang=eng&id=p39#p39)

2. Name six broad nodes in a plan-of-salvation learning diagram.
   - Guide: Creation, mortality, Christ’s redemption, resurrection, judgment, and eternal life; qualify the diagram as a model.
   - Sources: [Moses 1:39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/1?lang=eng&id=p39#p39); [2 Nephi 2:25–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/2?lang=eng&id=p25-p27#p25)

3. Why should two uncertain details be marked on such a diagram?
   - Guide: It makes the boundary between textual statements and inference visible and prevents speculative detail from becoming certainty.
   - Sources: [2 Nephi 2:25–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/2?lang=eng&id=p25-p27#p25)

4. How does Lehi connect moral agency with the Messiah?
   - Guide: Choice and freedom are described in relation to redemption rather than isolated self-sufficiency.
   - Sources: [2 Nephi 2:25–27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/2?lang=eng&id=p25-p27#p25)

5. Does agency mean that people choose all their circumstances?
   - Guide: No; moral action must be distinguished from control over circumstances and opportunity.
   - Sources: [Agency and Accountability](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/agency-and-accountability?lang=eng)

6. What direction does repentance take in the doctrine of Christ?
   - Guide: It involves turning toward God in relation to faith in Christ, not merely reporting sadness.
   - Sources: [2 Nephi 31:10–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/31?lang=eng&id=p10-p21#p10)

7. Name the foundational principles and ordinances in the path described by 2 Nephi 31.
   - Guide: Faith in Christ, repentance, baptism, receiving the Holy Ghost, and continuing steadfast discipleship.
   - Sources: [2 Nephi 31:10–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/31?lang=eng&id=p10-p21#p10)

8. Why is baptism not the end of the path in this passage?
   - Guide: The passage calls for continuing faithfulness and endurance after entry.
   - Sources: [2 Nephi 31:10–21](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/31?lang=eng&id=p10-p21#p10)

9. Distinguish a course checklist from a discipleship map.
   - Guide: One records assessable learning activities; the other depicts ongoing religious commitment that course XP cannot certify.
   - Sources: Original course design / source-analysis method.

10. What limit should a teacher observe when asking for evidence of learning?
   - Guide: Ask for explanation or analysis; do not require private confessions, testimony, or proof of spiritual worthiness.
   - Sources: Original course design / source-analysis method.

## Course 3: Life in Christ

### Reading guides

**Premortal life, creation, and the Fall**

- lds-doctrine-c03-m01-reading-01 — Abraham 3:22–28. Prompt: What does this passage contribute to the account of premortal life? Guide: Identify intelligences, divine purpose, and a mortal proving setting; distinguish textual claims from a complete biography of premortal individuals.
- lds-doctrine-c03-m01-reading-02 — Moses 4:1–12. Prompt: What choices and consequences structure this narrative? Guide: Trace competing purposes, temptation, and human action; avoid importing a detailed modern theory of psychology or science.

**Atonement, grace, and transformation**

- lds-doctrine-c03-m02-reading-01 — Alma 7:11–13. Prompt: Which kinds of suffering and help appear in this passage? Guide: Identify suffering, infirmity, death, and sin; explain the passage’s concern with Christ’s capacity to succor.
- lds-doctrine-c03-m02-reading-02 — 2 Nephi 2:6–9. Prompt: What grounds redemption in this passage? Guide: Locate redemption in the Messiah and his sacrifice; distinguish its grounding from the human response described.

**Baptism, confirmation, and covenant responsibility**

- lds-doctrine-c03-m03-reading-01 — Mosiah 18:8–10. Prompt: What responsibilities accompany the desire to be baptized? Guide: Identify bearing burdens, mourning and comforting, witnessing, and covenant service; explain their communal significance.
- lds-doctrine-c03-m03-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 20:77–79. Prompt: What covenant language appears in the sacrament prayers? Guide: Identify remembrance, willingness to take Christ’s name, keeping commandments, and the promised companionship of the Spirit; do not copy an entire liturgical text into the lesson.

**Revelation, discernment, and stewardship**

- lds-doctrine-c03-m04-reading-01 — Doctrine and Covenants 8:1–3. Prompt: How does this passage describe the mode of revelation? Guide: Describe mind and heart language while avoiding a universal diagnostic formula for every feeling.
- lds-doctrine-c03-m04-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 9:7–9. Prompt: What role does preparation play in this particular instruction? Guide: Identify study and asking in the translation setting; do not promise that every question receives an identical bodily signal.
- lds-doctrine-c03-m04-optional-reading-01 — Matthew 6:5–13. Prompt: What priorities and cautions shape prayer here? Guide: Identify sincerity, divine priorities, daily needs, forgiveness, and deliverance; distinguish an example prayer from a mechanical formula.

### Course checkpoint

1. Why should Abraham 3 and Moses 4 first be read separately?
   - Guide: Their settings and emphases differ; a synthesis should follow contextual reading rather than impose a single chronology.
   - Sources: [Abraham 3:22–28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/3?lang=eng&id=p22-p28#p22); [Moses 4:1–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&id=p1-p12#p1)

2. What does a creation narrative’s theological purpose fail to establish by itself?
   - Guide: A scientific mechanism, date, or exhaustive physical history; those require evidence of another kind.
   - Sources: [Moses 4:1–12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/4?lang=eng&id=p1-p12#p1)

3. Which aspects of suffering are addressed in Alma 7:11–13?
   - Guide: Suffering, infirmity, death, and sin in connection with Christ’s succoring role.
   - Sources: [Alma 7:11–13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/7?lang=eng&id=p11-p13#p11)

4. What grounds redemption in 2 Nephi 2:6–9?
   - Guide: The Messiah and his sacrifice; distinguish the ground of redemption from human response.
   - Sources: [2 Nephi 2:6–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/2?lang=eng&id=p6-p9#p6)

5. Why is a grace account incomplete if it portrays help as only a prize for previous achievement?
   - Guide: It obscures dependence on Christ; explain response and transformation without making self-sufficiency the basis.
   - Sources: [Grace](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/grace?lang=eng)

6. Name two communal responsibilities associated with baptism in Mosiah 18.
   - Guide: Any two of bearing burdens, mourning, comforting, witnessing, and covenant service, explained in context.
   - Sources: [Mosiah 18:8–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/18?lang=eng&id=p8-p10#p8)

7. Identify two commitments and a promised relationship in the sacrament prayers.
   - Guide: Remembrance and willingness to take Christ’s name or keep commandments, with the promised companionship of the Spirit.
   - Sources: [Doctrine and Covenants 20:77–79](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/20?lang=eng&id=p77-p79#p77)

8. Why can D&C 8’s mind-and-heart language not be used as an infallible diagnosis of every feeling?
   - Guide: It is a revelatory instruction in context, not a universal mechanical test establishing every experience’s origin.
   - Sources: [Doctrine and Covenants 8:1–3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/8?lang=eng&id=p1-p3#p1)

9. What role does study play in D&C 9?
   - Guide: Preparation and inquiry precede asking in the translation setting; avoid guaranteeing identical experiences for every question.
   - Sources: [Doctrine and Covenants 9:7–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/9?lang=eng&id=p7-p9#p7)

10. What is missing from a claim of personal revelation that directs another household?
   - Guide: Evidence of appropriate stewardship and respect for the other people’s agency; personal conviction alone does not supply authority.
   - Sources: [A Framework for Personal Revelation — Dale G. Renlund](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2022/10/14renlund?lang=eng)

## Course 4: Covenant Community and Eternal Hope

### Reading guides

**Priesthood, keys, authority, and service**

- lds-doctrine-c04-m01-reading-01 — Doctrine and Covenants 121:34–46. Prompt: What limits does this text place on coercive authority? Guide: Explain the contrast between unrighteous dominion and persuasion, patience, kindness, and love.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m01-reading-02 — Chapter 3: definitions of authority, keys, and power. Prompt: Which terms must remain distinct in an explanation? Guide: Define authority, keys, and power using the current chapter; cite section headings rather than inventing an office hierarchy.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m01-optional-reading-01 — Doctrine and Covenants 13:1. Prompt: What authority claim is made, and how should its historical setting be documented? Guide: State the narrated restoration claim and consult dated historical sources rather than equating faith affirmation with independent historical proof.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m01-optional-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 110:11–16. Prompt: What roles and keys are described in this account? Guide: Identify the named messengers and described responsibilities as the text presents them.

**Temple worship and covenants**

- lds-doctrine-c04-m02-reading-01 — Public overview: purpose and covenant descriptions. Prompt: How can an explanation stay substantive while respecting sacredness? Guide: Discuss publicly described purposes and covenants; avoid inventing or reproducing ceremony details.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m02-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 128:15–18. Prompt: How does this passage connect generations? Guide: Explain the intergenerational relationship and linking imagery without claiming that proxy actions compel acceptance.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m02-optional-reading-01 — Doctrine and Covenants 42:22–26. Prompt: What obligations concerning fidelity appear here? Guide: Explain fidelity and accountability without using the text to shame a learner or excuse coercion.

**Resurrection, judgment, and eternal life**

- lds-doctrine-c04-m03-reading-01 — 1 Corinthians 15:20–28. Prompt: What is Paul’s central claim about Christ and resurrection? Guide: Explain Christ’s resurrection and its relation to the dead; avoid treating these verses as a full LDS account of degrees of glory.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m03-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 76:50–70. Prompt: What characteristics and promises describe this group? Guide: Identify the passage’s descriptions and promises; distinguish its theological categories from judging named living individuals.

**Zion, consecration, and practical ethics**

- lds-doctrine-c04-m04-reading-01 — Moses 7:18. Prompt: Which communal characteristics define Zion here? Guide: Identify unity, righteousness, and absence of poverty; distinguish the ideal from a complete policy design.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m04-reading-02 — Mosiah 4:16–26. Prompt: How does the passage address need and limits on giving? Guide: Explain care for those in need and the text’s attention to circumstances; avoid shaming learners for limited resources.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m04-optional-reading-01 — Doctrine and Covenants 89:1–9. Prompt: What setting and instructions frame this revelation? Guide: Identify its stated setting and instructions; distinguish original wording from subsequent institutional application.
- lds-doctrine-c04-m04-optional-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 104:14–18. Prompt: How does stewardship connect resources and need? Guide: Explain stewardship and provision for the poor without claiming a complete modern economic blueprint.

### Course checkpoint

1. Distinguish priesthood keys from a priesthood office.
   - Guide: Keys concern directing authorized work within responsibilities; an office is not an interchangeable term or measure of personal worth.
   - Sources: [General Handbook, chapter 3: Priesthood Principles](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/3-priesthood-principles?lang=eng)

2. What does D&C 121 offer as an alternative to coercive dominion?
   - Guide: Persuasion, patience, kindness, love, and related qualities; explain accountable service.
   - Sources: [Doctrine and Covenants 121:34–46](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/121?lang=eng&id=p34-p46#p34)

3. What is an appropriate boundary for a public temple lesson?
   - Guide: Use publicly described purposes and covenants; do not reconstruct ceremonial details.
   - Sources: [About the Temple Endowment](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/temples/what-is-temple-endowment?lang=eng)

4. Does proxy participation mean someone is compelled to accept an ordinance?
   - Guide: No; explain the LDS account of choice and distinguish the rite from compelled acceptance.
   - Sources: [About the Temple Endowment](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/temples/what-is-temple-endowment?lang=eng)

5. How does D&C 128 connect generations?
   - Guide: Through an intergenerational relationship and linking imagery; do not make it a claim about forced outcomes.
   - Sources: [Doctrine and Covenants 128:15–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/128?lang=eng&id=p15-p18#p15)

6. Distinguish resurrection from exaltation.
   - Guide: Resurrection concerns renewed life after death; exaltation concerns a fuller LDS account of eternal life and relationship with God.
   - Sources: [1 Corinthians 15:20–28](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/1-cor/15?lang=eng&id=p20-p28#p20); [Becoming Like God](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/becoming-like-god?lang=eng)

7. Why should D&C 76 not be used to assign a neighbor an eternal destination?
   - Guide: It describes theological categories, while the learner lacks authority and knowledge to judge individuals.
   - Sources: [Doctrine and Covenants 76:50–70](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/76?lang=eng&id=p50-p70#p50)

8. What communal features define Zion in Moses 7:18?
   - Guide: Unity, righteousness, and absence of poverty, stated as the text’s ideal.
   - Sources: [Moses 7:18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/7?lang=eng&id=p18#p18)

9. How does Mosiah 4 complicate a service plan that shames people with limited resources?
   - Guide: It combines care for those in need with attention to circumstances; avoid equating limited resources with moral failure.
   - Sources: [Mosiah 4:16–26](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/4?lang=eng&id=p16-p26#p16)

10. Why does a religious ethic not by itself establish a complete public policy?
   - Guide: Policy also requires practical evidence, context, and expertise; distinguish principle from implementation.
   - Sources: [Moses 7:18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/7?lang=eng&id=p18#p18)

## Course 5: LDS Doctrine in Conversation

### Reading guides

**Godhead and Nicene Trinity in conversation**

- lds-doctrine-c05-m01-reading-01 — Catechism §§232–267: especially §§253–255. Prompt: Why is modalism an inaccurate account of the Trinity? Guide: Describe one God and distinct persons, rather than interchangeable appearances of one person.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m01-reading-02 — Nicene Creed: clauses about Father, Son, and Spirit. Prompt: Which claims concern the Son’s relation to the Father? Guide: Identify the creed’s own wording and its concern with shared divine nature; do not substitute LDS terminology.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m01-reading-03 — John 17:20–23. Prompt: How does the prayer’s concern with disciples affect reading “one”? Guide: Discuss unity and relationships in the prayer; avoid claiming this passage alone settles every later metaphysical dispute.

**Grace, faith, works, and justification**

- lds-doctrine-c05-m02-reading-01 — Augsburg Confession, Articles IV and VI. Prompt: How does the confession relate justification to new obedience? Guide: Explain the distinction between justification and the good works expected from faith; do not characterize Lutheranism as opposing moral action.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m02-reading-02 — Ephesians 2:8–10. Prompt: Why should verses 8–9 be read with verse 10? Guide: Connect gift language and good works while respecting the passage’s sequence.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m02-reading-03 — James 2:14–18. Prompt: What kind of faith claim is under examination? Guide: Identify a claim of faith disconnected from practical care and action; avoid turning the passage into an isolated slogan.

**Canon, revelation, and restoration claims**

- lds-doctrine-c05-m03-reading-01 — Articles of Faith 1:8–9. Prompt: What does this statement affirm about scripture and revelation? Guide: Identify the Bible, Book of Mormon, and continuing revelation; do not infer that every new opinion becomes canon.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m03-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 1:17–23. Prompt: How does this passage present its revelatory project? Guide: Describe its stated purpose and authority claims as claims within the text.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m03-reading-03 — Introduction and links to the 1832 and 1838 accounts. Prompt: What distinguishes the reported event from records of it? Guide: Separate the reported 1820 event from later written accounts and identify record dates and settings.

**Divine likeness, exaltation, and comparison limits**

- lds-doctrine-c05-m04-reading-01 — Becoming Like God: doctrinal explanation and cautions about speculation. Prompt: What boundaries should an explanation observe? Guide: Give the essay’s account in context and mark speculative details as unsupported; do not substitute popular caricatures.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m04-reading-02 — Romans 8:14–17. Prompt: How does familial and inheritance language function here? Guide: Explain children and heirs language in relation to Christ; do not claim it alone proves every later theology of exaltation.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m04-reading-03 — 2 Peter 1:3–8. Prompt: What ethical response accompanies participation language? Guide: Connect the passage’s divine-nature language with the listed virtues; avoid declaring all traditions’ interpretations identical.

**Religious freedom, conscience, and pluralism**

- lds-doctrine-c05-m05-reading-01 — Articles of Faith 1:11–12. Prompt: How do worship and civic responsibility appear together? Guide: Explain the claim of conscience and respect for others’ worship alongside civic duties; distinguish theological statements from legal advice.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m05-reading-02 — Doctrine and Covenants 134:4–5. Prompt: Which distinctions between religion and government matter here? Guide: Identify conscience and the passage’s view of civil authority; avoid inferring a complete modern constitutional doctrine.
- lds-doctrine-c05-m05-reading-03 — Alma 1:17–18. Prompt: What tension between belief and conduct appears in this narrative? Guide: Distinguish differing beliefs from actions governed by law; discuss the narrative rather than applying it as a modern penal code.

### Course checkpoint

1. What must a fair LDS–Nicene comparison do with “person” and “unity”?
   - Guide: Define each tradition’s terms from its own sources and acknowledge that they do not map exactly.
   - Sources: [Godhead](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/godhead?lang=eng); [Catechism of the Catholic Church, §§232–267](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P17.HTM)

2. What question is central to the Nicene clauses about the Son’s relation to the Father?
   - Guide: The Son’s divine relation and nature; answer from the creed rather than substituting LDS definitions.
   - Sources: [The Nicene Creed — The Orthodox Faith](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/doctrine-scripture/the-symbol-of-faith/nicene-creed)

3. Why does John 17 alone not settle all later metaphysical disagreements?
   - Guide: Its prayer context supplies relational and unity language, but later systems involve additional interpretation and commitments.
   - Sources: [John 17:20–23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/17?lang=eng&id=p20-p23#p20)

4. How do Augsburg Articles IV and VI relate justification and obedience?
   - Guide: Distinguish justification from the expected good works of living faith; do not claim Lutheranism rejects moral action.
   - Sources: [Augsburg Confession, Articles IV and VI](https://bookofconcord.org/augsburg-confession/)

5. Why read Ephesians 2:10 with 2:8–9?
   - Guide: It places good works alongside gift language, preventing an incomplete excerpt-based contrast.
   - Sources: [Ephesians 2:8–10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/eph/2?lang=eng&id=p8-p10#p8)

6. What claim about faith does James 2 interrogate?
   - Guide: Faith claimed without practical action or care; identify its argument rather than a slogan.
   - Sources: [James 2:14–18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/james/2?lang=eng&id=p14-p18#p14)

7. Distinguish open canon from the claim that every new opinion is scripture.
   - Guide: Continuing revelation does not automatically confer canonical status on every individual interpretation.
   - Sources: [Articles of Faith 1:8–9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/a-of-f/1?lang=eng&id=p8-p9#p8)

## Capstone scoring

A qualified human reviewer or a validated assessment workflow must evaluate the rubric. Self-checking completion does not constitute a pass. A course facilitator should have documented familiarity with LDS doctrine, source criticism, and respectful cross-tradition comparison.

**Accuracy and context**

- 0: Major distortions or unsupported doctrinal claims.
- 1: Mostly accurate but important context missing.
- 2: Accurate, contextual account with minor gaps.
- 3: Precise account with context and clearly qualified limits.

**Traceable sources**

- 0: Claims largely untraceable.
- 1: Some sources but unclear attribution or inappropriate support.
- 2: Major claims traceable to appropriate sources.
- 3: Claim-level trail distinguishes perspectives, settings, and limits.

**Fair alternative**

- 0: No serious alternative, or a caricature.
- 1: Alternative named but weakly represented.
- 2: Serious alternative represented and reasonably answered.
- 3: Strong alternative used to test and improve the thesis.

**Coherent synthesis and limitations**

- 0: Incoherent argument or no meaningful synthesis.
- 1: Recognizable thesis but weak integration or missing limits.
- 2: Coherent synthesis with practical application and explicit limits.
- 3: Well-integrated, accessible synthesis with responsible application and a clear revision trail.

Pass at 9/12, with every criterion at least 1. If failed, identify specific revisions and allow resubmission. Keep reviewer ID, review date, rubric scores, and revision version with the completion event. Award XP once after a pass.

## Minimum reviewer calibration

Before assessing learners, reviewers should independently score three sample answers, compare disagreements, and document the agreed application of the rubric. Review accurate minority interpretations on their evidence rather than penalizing them for disagreeing with a facilitator. Escalate doctrinal ambiguity for editorial review. Automated scoring needs validation against such reviewed samples; this package does not provide a validated grading engine.
